💡 How Range Multipliers Work: When a product's wholesale cost falls inside a specific range, CreditLog automatically applies that range's multiplier to calculate the retail price. Low-cost items (e.g. $0.50 – $2.00) get higher multipliers (e.g. 2.50x) to ensure strong gross profits, while high-value products ($40+) use lower multipliers (e.g. 1.20x) to stay competitive.
